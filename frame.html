The frame is not
the variable.

Most chassis in a given class will win races. What separates them is whether the tubing and axle suit the power you're making, and whether anyone within driving distance stocks parts on a Saturday. This tool filters on both. It does not rank.

What this can't tell you

Which chassis is winning at your home track, and who shows up with a parts trailer. That decides more than anything on this page. Call the track.

On missing specs

Blank fields mean the builder doesn't publish the number, not that the kart is worse. Several of the strongest 206 chassis sell on results, not spec sheets.

Prices

Rolling chassis, from dealer listings, current as of build. They move. Confirm before you order.

How the checkers work

Compliance runs red to gold — red is a stiff frame built for two-stroke power, gold is one that flexes enough to rotate on nine horsepower.
