If you're new

Four-cycle LO206 is where most adults and most kids should start, and the reason is not price — it's that nine horsepower forces you to learn the things that actually make a driver fast. You cannot cover a mistake with power. Every tenth comes from line, minimum steering input, and not scrubbing speed you already paid for.

The equipment questions come second, and they matter less than the internet suggests. In any given class, half a dozen chassis will win races. What actually separates them is whether the frame suits the power you're making, and whether someone in your paddock can hand you a spindle on a Saturday.
