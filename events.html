Two different worlds, both called karting

The recreation and family-entertainment side buys rental fleets, timing systems and barriers. The competition side buys chassis, engines, tyres and data. They rarely share a room, and someone arriving from one can be genuinely surprised by the other — so both are listed here rather than pretending it's one industry.

Note that PRI is trade-only and needs industry credentials. IAAPA and Amusement Expo are industry events too, though more accessible to operators. The race weeks are open to spectators, and for a family working out whether this sport is for them, standing in the SuperNationals paddock for a day teaches more than any amount of reading.
