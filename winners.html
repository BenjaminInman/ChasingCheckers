How to read this, and how not to

Every row comes from a published race report and links back to it. Where a result was provisional at the time of the report, or changed in technical inspection, it says so on the row.

The tally counts main event wins by chassis brand, and it is not a ranking of which chassis is fastest. Entry numbers vary wildly by brand — a marque with thirty karts in a class will collect more wins than one with three, without being any quicker. Team quality, engine builder and driver matter more than the frame in most of these results. What the tally is genuinely good for is telling you which equipment has real support in the national paddock, because that is what determines whether you can get parts and setup help at your track.

Scope right now is the national series — SKUSA Pro Tour and USPKS. Regional and club racing is where most people actually race, and it's not covered here yet. Sprint karting only, no dirt or road racing.
