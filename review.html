Facilities & manufacturers

Put it in front of us.

If you build karting equipment or run a facility and you want it covered, tell us about it. We race what we write about and we spend our own money doing it — so we'll say what we actually think, and we'll say plainly how we got hold of whatever we're reviewing.

What we cover

Facilities

Outdoor sprint tracks, indoor rental centres, kartplexes and race shops. What the surface is like, what the paddock is like on a race weekend, whether a newcomer would be looked after or left standing in the pits.

Karting hardware

Chassis, engine packages, tyres, seats, data systems, tools and trailer kit. Anything a club racer or a parent actually has to buy.

Driver equipment

Suits, helmets, ribs, gloves, boots. Especially youth sizing, which is chronically under-covered and where the fit problems are real.

Software & services

Timing, data analysis, coaching programmes, sim hardware and arrive-and-drive operations.

Send it over

We read everything. We reply to what we can realistically cover.

How we work

No guaranteed outcome

Sending us something buys a look, not a verdict. If it doesn't hold up we'll say so, and we'd rather tell you that before you ship anything than after.

Everything gets disclosed

Loaned, gifted, comped or bought — it's stated in the review. US rules require disclosing a material connection, and we'd do it regardless.

No paid reviews

We don't sell coverage and we don't let anyone approve copy before it runs. Sponsorship, if we ever take it, stays clearly separate from what we review.

Real use, not unboxing

We'd rather run something for a season than film it once. That means we're slow, and it means what we publish is worth reading.

Where we are

Based near Nashville and racing in the region, so facility visits are easiest in the Southeast. Further afield is possible, just slower.

What helps

Specs, pricing and who it's aimed at. If there's an existing product it's meant to beat, name it — comparisons make for far better coverage than solo impressions.
